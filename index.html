Mastering Recount Text

Pelajari struktur dan pemahaman mendalam mengenai teks pengalaman masa lampau (Recount Text), kemudian uji kemampuan berpikir tingkat tinggi (HOTS) kamu!

1

Identify Generic Structure

Recount Text adalah teks yang menceritakan kembali peristiwa atau pengalaman yang terjadi di masa lampau secara kronologis. Strukturnya terdiri dari:

  • 1. Orientation: Pengenalan tokoh (who), tempat (where), dan waktu terjadinya peristiwa (when).
  • 2. Events: Rangkaian kejadian atau peristiwa yang diceritakan secara berurutan sesuai waktu.
  • 3. Re-orientation: Kesimpulan, rangkuman, atau kesan/perasaan penulis di akhir cerita (bersifat opsional).
2

Comprehend Recount Texts

Untuk memahami isi dan makna tersirat dalam Recount Text, perhatikan fungsi sosial dan ciri kebahasaannya:

Social Function: Memberikan informasi atau menghibur pembaca melalui pengalaman nyata/imajinatif di masa lalu.
Language Features:
  • Menggunakan Simple Past Tense (went, saw, realized, felt).
  • Menggunakan Time Connectives (first, then, after that, finally).
  • Menggunakan Action Verbs dan keterangan waktu lampau (last holiday, two years ago).

An Unforgettable Lesson at Mount Sibayak

[Paragraph 1] Last semester break, my cousin Ardi and I decided to hike Mount Sibayak in North Sumatra. Since we had hiked several hills before, we arrogantly assumed that Sibayak would be an easy trail. We packed lightly, bringing only one small bottle of water each, a light jacket, and no flashlight, as we planned to descend before noon.

[Paragraph 2] We started our ascent at 6:00 AM under a clear sky. For the first two hours, everything went smoothly, and we joked about how overprepared other hikers looked with their heavy backpacks. However, shortly after we reached the rocky crater area at 9:30 AM, thick fog suddenly rolled in, dropping the temperature drastically. Within minutes, visibility shrunk to less than three meters, and the familiar trail vanished beneath the mist.

[Paragraph 3] Panic began to set in as our phone batteries died from the cold. Realizing that wandering around could lead us toward steep cliffs, we remembered a basic survival rule: stay put and conserve energy. We huddled behind a large volcanic rock to shield ourselves from the freezing wind. Almost two hours later, we heard voices calling out. Fortunately, a group of experienced local guides noticed our absence at the basecamp post and came looking for us. They shared warm tea, lent us thermal blankets, and guided us safely back down.

[Paragraph 4] That terrifying afternoon completely changed my perspective on nature. I realized that overconfidence can be far more dangerous than a difficult trail, and proper preparation is never something to mock.
